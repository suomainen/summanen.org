Cookies and privacy

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser. This page explains what summanen.org stores and why.

Cookies on this site

Under section 205 of the Finnish Act on Electronic Communications Services (917/2014) (opens in a new tab, Finlex, in Finnish), I may store cookies on your device if they are strictly necessary for the site to work. For all other cookies I need your consent.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity cookies are stored only if you accept them. Google Analytics counts visitors. Clarity shows how the site is used: click heatmaps and session recordings. If you decline, the site does not load either service at all.

There are no forms on the site. Contact happens by email or phone, and the site does not store your contact details.

Privacy: Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity

If you accept cookies, I use Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics to see how the site is used. Clarity records clicks, scrolling and mouse movements, which produce heatmaps and session recordings. Google Analytics counts visitors and shows, for example, which devices people use and where they come from.

I use the data only to improve the content and usability of the site, not for advertising or for identifying individual visitors.

The data is collected with cookies and similar technologies (see the table above). Microsoft and Google process the data as the providers of their services, and it may also be processed outside the EU. More information: Microsoft Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab) and Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the Analytics cookies switch at the top of this page. Tracking then stops and the site’s cookies are removed.
